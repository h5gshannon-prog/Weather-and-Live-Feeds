Google Calendar

private to your browser

Your calendar must be set to public for this to work — Google Calendar's embed feature can't show a private calendar just because you're logged in elsewhere on the page.

  1. In Google Calendar, go to Settings (gear icon) → click your calendar under "Settings for my calendars".
  2. Under Access permissions, check "Make available to public".
  3. Scroll down to "Integrate calendar" and copy the Calendar ID — for most personal calendars this is just your Gmail address.
  4. Paste that ID above and click Load calendar.

The ID you enter is saved only in this browser (localStorage) — it isn't sent anywhere else by this page.
